01 / ABOUT

I study the physics of extreme environments.

I am a computational (astro)physicist and Richard P. Feynman Distinguished Postdoctoral Fellow at Los Alamos National Laboratory, based at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor. My work spans interdisciplinary research in inertial confinement fusion, high-energy astrophysics and large scale evolution of the Universe.

This is a deliberately short first version of my website. More about my current research, projects, and talks is coming soon.

02 / RESEARCH

01

Inertial Confinement Fusion

Applying explainable machine learning to numerical simulations of inertial confinement fusion experiments to optimise future target designs in double-shell ICF.

02

High-energy astrophysics

Understanding energetic phenomena and the physical processes operating in extreme astrophysical environments.

03

Compact objects

Using observations and physical models to investigate accretion, black holes, and neutron stars.
